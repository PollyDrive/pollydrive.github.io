  + каждый акт наблюдения формирует структуру реальности

  + Эмоциональные реакции на других людей и ситуации — это отражение ваших собственных подавленных качеств и убеждений (проекция).

  + Чувство благодарности притягивает позитивные события.

  + Чувство обиды или негодования усиливает восприятие несправедливости.

  + The psychological truth that your mind will externalize whatever it can't accept internally. So for example, **when you judge someone for** being arrogant, you most likely feel that **others are going to judge you** if you're confident too, which is why you aren't. It's usually your own **unexpressed confidence trying to be seen**.

  + When **you feel intimidated by someone's success**, you probably believe that **you will intimidate people if you become successful**, too, which is probably **why you aren't**.

  + When **you can't stand people who seek attention** or create content, it's often because **you've denied your own need to be seen**. You've learned to equate visibility with ego, so you hide and fake humility and resent those who express what you secretly want permission to do.

  + When **you judge people for chasing status or money**, you might be projecting your own buried ambition and believe that others will judge you if you chase status or money. You've convinced yourself that wanting more is shallow, so **you sabotage your own drive and criticize it in others to feel morally safe**.

  + When you keep attracting unreliable people, it's often reflecting that you are unreliable with people or with yourself.
